Privacy Policy

Application: Neon Pinball: Retro Arcade (com.aistudio.pinball.retro)
Last Updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Neon Pinball: Retro Arcade mobile application (the "App"), developed and distributed as an ad-supported arcade game on the Google Play Store.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This document explains what information is processed when you play Neon Pinball, how third-party advertising partners utilize data, and what rights and controls you have.

1. Information Collection & Usage

Summary: Neon Pinball does NOT require account registration, login credentials, name, email address, physical location, contact lists, or microphone/camera access. We do not personally collect or store your personal information on any private servers.

A. Local Data Storage on Device:

  • High Scores & Leaderboard: Your high scores, table multipliers, and game statistics are stored exclusively in an offline database on your local device.
  • User Preferences: Audio toggle states (sound FX on/off) and vibration/haptic feedback settings are saved locally on your device.
  • This information never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server by our application.

B. Third-Party Advertising Services:

Neon Pinball uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements (banner ads, interstitial ads between games, and optional rewarded ads).

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data in accordance with Google's policies, which may include:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) to serve relevant advertising and measure ad performance.
  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and system language.
  • Coarse Location: General region/country derived from IP address for localized ad serving.
  • Ad Interaction & Analytics: Views, clicks, impressions, and diagnostic data regarding ad delivery.

2. Third-Party Privacy Policies

For complete information on how Google collects, uses, and safeguards information, please review Google's official policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob Privacy and Security

3. Advertising Choices & Opt-Out

You can easily control or opt out of personalized advertisements directly through your Android device settings at any time:

  • Open your device's Settings app.
  • Navigate to Google → All Services (if applicable) → Ads.
  • Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • You can also enable Opt out of Ads Personalization on supported Android versions.

Note: If you opt out of personalized ads, you will still see advertisements in the game, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Neon Pinball is designed for general audiences. The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ad services without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate measures.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all game scores, settings, and player data are stored exclusively on your device:

  • You can delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the app data via Settings → Apps → Neon Pinball → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally stored data.

6. Security

We value your trust. The App communicates with the Google Mobile Ads SDK using encrypted HTTPS protocols. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
